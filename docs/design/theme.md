# Theme: The WeddingOS Design Contract

> **This document is the ultimate source of truth for the visual identity of WeddingOS.**
> All UI/UX decisions must be validated against these principles.

## 1. Product Mood & Identity

WeddingOS is designed to be an emotional anchor in a chaotic process. It is a decision-support system, not a marketplace or a project management tool.

### Core Attributes
- **Calm**: The interface never shouts. It whispers. It reduces anxiety by removing clutter and cognitive load.
- **Elegant**: While functional, it feels premium and trustworthy. It respects the gravity of the occasion.
- **Human**: The copy and interactions feel like a supportive friend, not a robot or a corporate form.
- **Emotionally Safe**: Errors are gentle suggestions. Success is celebrated but not gamified.
- **Brazilian Context**: Colors, tone, and imagery deeply resonate with Brazilian warmth and celebration culture without being stereotypical or carnival-esque.

## 2. Design Philosophy

- **Clarity Over Completeness**: We do not show every option at once. We curate the information to help the user make the *next* decision, not *all* decisions.
- **Cards Over Tables**: Tables are for accountants. Cards are for humans comparing emotional choices.
- **No Visual Noise**: Every pixel must earn its place. If it doesn't help the decision, remove it. Borders are thin or non-existent.
- **Decision Support, Not Automation**: We help the couple decide; we don't decide for them. We provide clarity, comparison, and pros/cons.

## 3. Strict Visual Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1.  **NO Pure Black (`#000000`)**: Use deep charcoals, slates, or midnight blues to soften contrast.
2.  **NO Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) Backgrounds**: Use "warm paper" or "off-white" tones to reduce eye strain and feeling of sterility.
3.  **NO Gradients**: Flat, solid colors build trust and clarity. Gradients add unnecessary noise.
4.  **NO Aggressive Colors**: Red is for "Action required", not "CRITICAL FATAL ERROR".
5.  **NO Heavy Shadows**: Elevation is indicated by subtle, diffused shadows or slight border color changes. Hard drop shadows are forbidden.
6.  **Rounded Corners Only**: Sharp 90-degree corners are "enterprise software". We use `8px`, `12px`, or `16px` radii to feel organic and approachable.
7.  **Mobile-First Always**: Every screen must be designed for a 375px width first. Desktop is an adaptation, not the primary target.

## 4. Color Strategy

Colors in WeddingOS are functional and emotional, not just decorative.

- **Green (Primary Action)**: Represents growth, "go", and natural progress. It is the color of moving forward (e.g., "Schedule Visit", "Confirm Date").
- **Warm Neutrals (Backgrounds)**: Sand, deeply reduced opacity oranges/yellows. Creates the "paper" feel of high-end wedding stationery.
- **Warning vs. Error**:
    - **Warning (Yellow/Orange)**: "Take a look at this, it might impact your budget." (Advisory)
    - **Error (Soft Red/Rose)**: "We couldn't save that." (Gentle correction, never scolding).
- **Text Colors**: Dark grey for headings, medium grey for body. Never pure black.

## 5. Typography Rules

- **Sans-Serif Only**: We use modern, geometric or humanist sans-serifs (e.g., Inter, Figtree) for maximum legibility on mobile screens.
- **High Readability**: Line height is generous (1.5 or greater). Paragraphs are short.
- **NO Decorative Fonts**: Script fonts are cliché in the wedding industry. We avoid them to maintain clarity and modernity.
- **Calm Hierarchy**: Font size differences are subtle. We use weight (Medium vs Regular) more than size to distinguish hierarchy.

## 6. Interaction & Motion

- **Subtle**: Animations should be felt, not seen. A card "gliding" into place, not "flying" or "bouncing".
- **Predictable**: Interactions happen exactly as the user expects. Standard patterns are preferred over clever ones.
- **Never Playful or Flashy**: No confetti explosions unless explicitly celebrating a MAJOR milestone (like "Wedding Date Set"). No wobbling buttons or aggressive hover states.

## 7. What WeddingOS is NOT

- **NOT a Fintech**: We deal with budgets, but we don't look like a bank.
- **NOT a SaaS Dashboard**: No dense sidebars, no complex data tables, no "admin panel" vibes.
- **NOT a Productivity App**: No "checkbox fatigue". We focus on quality of decisions, not quantity of tasks.
- **NOT a Social Network**: No concept of "likes", "shares", or "feeds". This is a private space for the couple.
